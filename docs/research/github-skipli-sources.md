## Visual-art direction

The following files are generated conceptual covers. They are not screenshots and must not be presented as literal product interfaces:

- `public/media/novachat-cover-v1.png` — modular dark system with an orange message path; represents reliable real-time messaging architecture.
- `public/media/platforms-manage-cover-v1.png` — editorial calendar, media and approval forms; represents coordinated content operations.
- `public/media/squareup-cover-v1.png` — connected order, menu, service and receipt forms; represents restaurant operations and ordering.

All three covers follow the Kamling visual language: graphite or warm off-white materials, a single orange accent, generous negative space, no logos, no readable product UI, and no fabricated metrics.
